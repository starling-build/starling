# amd-10 — glyph-gap check of the Windows metrics tables (2026-11-20, for starling-bd)

Trigger: e466671e (PR #287) lost 3.5 pt a line because Arial Narrow had no U+201D entry (fell back to the 0.333 em stand-in; Word prints 0.273).
Method: script lists, for every `[UInt32: Double]` advance table in OfficeFonts.swift, which of U+2018-201F, 2013, 2014, 2026, 00A0, 00AB, 00BB, 00BF, 00A1, 20AC and Latin-1 letters C0-FF have NO entry. A missing entry only matters when the stand-in font's width differs from the Windows face. Script: /data/s/amd-10/x/gap/chk.py, full output out.txt (53 tables).

Script-specific tables (Greek, Cyrillic, Hebrew, Thai, Calibri alnum) are missing nearly everything by design: Latin goes through another path. Ignore those.

Latin tables with real gaps (the ones to measure on Windows):
- Arial Narrow regular: 2018, 201A-201F except 201C/201D now, 2013, 2014, 00A0, 00AB, 00BB, 00BF, 00A1, 20AC, 9 Latin-1 letters. Bold: same, plus 7 of 8 quotes.
- Segoe UI regular+bold: all 8 quotes, 2013, 2014, 2026, 20AC.
- Tahoma Latin regular+bold: all quotes, 2013, 2014, 2026, 20AC.
- Montserrat regular+bold: all quotes, 2013, 2014, 2026, 20AC.
- Nunito Sans (Light/Regular/Bold): 4 of 8 quotes, 20AC.
- Georgia regular+bold: 1 quote. Verdana regular+bold: 2 quotes. Aptos regular+bold: 1 quote, 00A0.
- Franklin Gothic Medium Cond: nearly everything beyond ASCII.
- thSarabun/Angsana/Browallia Latin: quotes, dashes, 00A0, Latin-1.
- Lucida Sans Unicode: complete.
