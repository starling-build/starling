VERDICT: #266's rule was right; its line fitting was wrong. In all four docs Word honours the stop past the margin AND keeps the text on the line. Not one case in the lab or the docs shows Word wrapping or clamping at compat <= 14. The "text fits before the stop" gate in the wip diff is not a Word property; drop it.

RULE (Windows pages, compat < 15, every paragraph: justified, headers, footers and cells too):
R1. An explicit tab stop past the right margin (or past the cell edge) is used at its own position, whatever the alignment (left, centre, right), with or without a leader. Nothing is clamped. There is no "how far past" threshold: drop the 0.5 test in pixels. It is scale-dependent, and it flipped gv's +3 twip stop between clamped and honoured.
R2. A tab that starts at or past the margin, with no explicit stop left, goes to the next default stop (a multiple of defaultTabStop), even past the margin.
R3. The text after such a tab, up to the next tab or the end of the line, stays on the line. Its part past the margin does not count against the line. For breaking, it counts as if it ended at the margin; when drawn, it sits where its stop puts it (right: ends at the stop; centre: centred on it; left: starts at it). Later text on the same line moves with it.

WHY #266 FAILED (all four drops are the same mechanism): the honoured path made the text after the tab end past the margin, and the engine's line fit is against the margin. The engine can only hang one character's advance (amd-9's note), so:
- 0058af10's "(*)" went to its own line;
- e1996ca7's "119.6" went to its own line, a row late for the rest of the page;
- 9f798d64's "asseblief" went to the next line;
- in gv, honouring the +3 stop put the second tab's start past the margin, so "2" wrapped, the footer grew a line, and page 2's body lost 2 lines.

IMPLEMENTATION HINT: before #266, the clamped layout broke lines exactly where Word does in all four docs (88/91, 219/221, 300/304 lines matched; 9f798d64's footer and header rows matched in y). So keep the clamped geometry for BREAKING and add a draw-time shift of the tab's segment, equal to Word's position minus the clamped one, applied to the rest of the line. Then R2 applies from the shifted x, which gives gv's "2" at the 7920 default stop. Left and centre stops past the margin need the same treatment (today they are clamped, or wrap as at compat 15).

STILL UNKNOWN (not needed for these four docs): where Word breaks when the text after a stop past the margin is wider than the room up to the stop. A right tab then has no width, and the text runs past the stop. If an implementation has to choose, the ONE lab is compat 14 with tabpast14's margins:
- right, centre and left stops at +300;
- the tab starting 30 pt short of the margin;
- multi-word text after the tab, about 20, 40 and 80 pt wide;
- the same cases in a justified paragraph;
- a tab after the last stop with the text inside the margin, where the next default stop is past it.
