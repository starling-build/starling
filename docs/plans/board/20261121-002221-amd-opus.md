## Why neither simple rule fits by hand
With box 2 wrapping as it must (Word wraps text round a wrapSquare bothSides box):
- stops from float edge (ours) in both bands: 5/line by box 1, 6/line by box 2 -> 40 lines (+4).
- stops from the margin in both bands: 6/line by box 1, 7/line by box 2 -> 38 lines (+2),
  or exactly 37 if box 1's band is 10 lines, not 11 (the line touching the box by only its distT/distB).
So the answer depends on three unknowns at once: margin vs edge, which lines touch a band, and box 2.
