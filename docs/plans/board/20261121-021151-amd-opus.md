# PLAN amd-opus puzzle 17: the Symbol-bullet row at a line multiple (sa1 03f63881, 5e813226, lab bl4490)

Analysis only, no code. Word prints read with fitz; ours from amd-8's f861a5629 binary via wordcmp.
Face metrics: /data/corpus-cache/metrics/latin/*.tsv (winAscent / winDescent / hhea, unitsPerEm 2048).

## The rule (property, not doc)

A Symbol bullet makes its line taller by D = max(0, B - T), all of it ABOVE the baseline:
- B = Symbol's winAscent 2059/2048 = 1.00537 em x the bullet's size. Bullet size = the paragraph mark's
  own size, else the paragraph style chain's, else docDefaults. Never the first run's.
- T = the text's SINGLE-SPACED top-to-baseline: winAscent + GDI external leading, over unitsPerEm, x the run's
  size. External leading = max(0, hheaLineGap - ((winAsc + winDesc) - (hheaAsc + |hheaDesc|))).
  Tahoma 1.0005 em, Calibri 0.9521, Arial 0.9380 (1854 + 67), Times 0.933. Take the tallest face drawn on the line.
- D is NOT scaled by the multiple, and Symbol's descent (0.2197 em) adds nothing below.
- The bullet line's baseline drops by D. The line after it keeps the normal pitch.

The same holds at single spacing (5e813226 p8: Calibri 12, D 0.64; Word +0.60 in, row +0.62 to 0.72).

## Evidence (Word's print; "in" = pitch into the bullet line, "out" = pitch from it to the next line)

| case | D by the rule | Word row change | Word in / out vs plain |
|---|---|---|---|
| lab bl4490 a/d, Arial 12 + Symbol 12, 1.15 | 0.809 | +0.84 | +0.84 / 0.00 |
| 5e813226 p37, Calibri 12 + Symbol 12, 1.15 (14 one-line items) | 0.639 | +0.635 | +0.76 / -0.09 |
| 03f63881, Tahoma 12 + Symbol 12, 1.15 (print scale 0.752; 37 in, 64 out, 243 plain) | 0.059 | +0.043 | +0.117 / -0.074 (unscaled) |

Rejected: scaling D by the multiple (Calibri would be +0.73, Arial +0.93). Arial's bare winAscent with no
line gap (Arial would be +1.20). The multiple-spaced baseline. Counting Symbol's descent (Tahoma would be +0.22).

The in/out split is off by up to 0.16 pt, and it is real. Word puts the baseline a whole number of 1/600-inch
dots below the line top: round(ascent x ppem), with ppem = size x 600/72. The bullet line therefore drops by the
ROUNDED difference: Tahoma 101 - 100 = 1 dot (0.12 pt), Calibri 101 - 95 = 6 (0.72), Arial 101 - 94 = 7 (0.84).
The row grows only by the exact D, so the next line comes closer by the remainder. This is under 0.1 pt and does
not add up down the page. It is optional.

## Why our rule is wrong, and why amd-2's fix dropped

1. Our rule measures T as `m.baseline / multiple` of an "x" probe, which uses the STAND-IN face's ascent. That is
   right for Arial and Calibri, so lab bl4490 and 5e813226 p37 rows match (ours 17.48, Word's average 17.48).
   For Tahoma it gives 0.984 x the real ascent, so D comes out 0.25 pt instead of 0.06. The row's above/below
   also comes from the probe, so the extra lands BELOW the line: ours in 12.54 / out 12.73, Word 12.64 / 12.50.
   Fix: take T from the real face's metrics, and put D above, keeping the line's own below-baseline part.
2. amd-2's `own = max(own, faceAscent x size)` fixed D (pairs 25.11 vs Word 25.13) but uncovered two short
   errors that the excess was cancelling. Removing them explains the page-17 drift of -0.65 pt:
   - The plain Tahoma 12 pitch at 1.15: ours 12.540 printed (16.676 pt), Word 12.554 (16.694 pt) over
     243 pitches. That is -0.014 x about 30 lines = -0.42 pt.
   - The two-line bold "Criterios" headings: Word 25.22, ours 25.08. That is -0.14 x 2 = -0.28 pt.
   Together they make -0.70 pt, against the -0.65 amd-2 saw. So fix 1 must land WITH the plain-pitch fix,
   or after it. It cannot land alone.

## The plain-pitch lead (H2, needs a check before anyone builds on it)

Word's single line may be round(winAscent x ppem / upm) + round(extLeading ...) + round(winDescent x ppem / upm)
whole 600-dpi dots:
- Tahoma 12 = 100 + 21 = 121 dots = 14.52 pt. At 1.15 that is 16.698; Word 16.694, ours 16.676, em-exact 16.657.
- Calibri 12 = 95 + 27 = 122 dots, Arial 12 = 91 + 3 + 21 = 115 dots (13.80). Both agree with what we
  already match.
- Sharp tests where H2 and em-exact differ: Tahoma 10 should be 100 dots = 12.00 pt single (em-exact 12.07),
  Tahoma 11 = 111 dots = 13.32 (13.28). Check plain Tahoma pitches in the corpus, or in a lab at 9, 10, 11, 12 pt,
  single and 1.15, before using H2. If it holds it is a general line-height rule, so it needs a wide A/B.
