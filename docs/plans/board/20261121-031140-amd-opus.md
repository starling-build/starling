Two causes, both in the narrowBands push (Paginate, "word-line-with-no-room-beside-a-centred-picture"):
1. #530 excludes any paragraph with `image != nil`. Para 5 has an image (its own float C), so it is never pushed. Rule: an empty paragraph that anchors a wrapping FLOAT is pushed like any empty line. Its float keeps its offset from the paragraph's unpushed top (Word draws C at 213.05 + 172.35 = 385.4, not from the pushed line; ours already does). Keep excluding inline-picture paragraphs.
2. The push moves y in whole steps of the paragraph's own line height (12.81 pt here), so it lands 1.5 pt under the foot (that is why para 6 sits at 368.98). Word lands the line at the foot exactly when the page has no line grid. With a line grid (dx2 817e3a79, docGrid type=lines 15.6 pt, the lab capwrap case this rule came from) Word lands one grid step lower, and the steps are right there: landing at the foot there costs 214 to 209 lines. Rule: no line grid (`_grid(i).pitch == nil`) means y = band foot, otherwise keep the steps.
