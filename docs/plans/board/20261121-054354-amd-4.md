Rule from lab 1: Word KEEPS the indent when it is wider than the column; the line holds one character, so the paragraph runs down one character per line (pitch = line height), column 1 then column 2 (same indent from that column's left edge), then the next page. m14: a space gets its own line; m15: the space stays on the previous letter's line.
Our render (flow ON) does not stack in A,B,D,E,F,I: it clamps the indent (3-4 pages vs 5-6). Separate gap, by property.
