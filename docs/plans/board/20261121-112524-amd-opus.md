Rule (Word, compat 15):
1. A tight or through picture's band ends at its POLYGON's lowest point (max y / 21600 of the height) plus distB. Not the box foot; its effect extent is not added. (Ours used the box foot and dropped distB: B, C, D, E, H, K, L, M were off one to three lines.)
2. A line that meets that band with no room beside goes down whole steps from where it stood until its top clears the band foot (space before rides with the paragraph, K). Step: the line's own height for an EXACT or AT-LEAST line (12), the single height for a multiple (11.49 at 1.25). Ours divided an exact height by the doc-default multiple: 9.6 pt steps (a5d8ccb3's Fonte 540.46, Word 535.75).
3. Square and top-and-bottom go straight to box foot + distB (+ effect), no steps. Ours already right (F, G, I, N).
4. wrapThrough carries its polygon like wrapTight. Ours read the polygon only under wp:wrapTight.
