# SPEC (amd-opus, planner) for lenovo-2: line height of a line holding OMML math

Labs: lenovo-2's frac3 and frac4 (compat 12), plus amd-opus's mathline
(/data/s/amd-opus/starling/lab/mathline/, compat 15 and 12, both printed).
**Compat 12 and 15 print identically**, so one rule covers both modes.
Word positions are quantised to 0.12 pt. Every number below fits Word within 0.12 pt
unless marked otherwise. Cambria Math MATH constants are per 2048 units.

## 1. An equation's line extent (single spacing)
Let s = the equation's size (its base run size, or the ctrlPr size of a stacked object).
- **ascent** = max of:
  - the text runs' ascents;
  - each plain math run at base level: **0.950 · its size** (Cambria's win ascent, not Cambria Math's 0.779);
  - the formula's ink top + **MathLeading 300/2048 · s = 0.1465 s**.
- **descent** = max of:
  - the text runs' descents;
  - each plain base-level math run: **0.222 · its size**;
  - the formula's ink bottom (no leading below).
- Numerator and denominator boxes, scripts and limits count only through their **ink**, never through a font box.
- **Ink** means glyph bounding boxes as OpenType MATH lays them out. A radical adds
  **RadicalExtraAscender 133/2048 · s** above its bar (C16: bar top 17.66 + 1.30 + 2.93 = 21.89; Word 21.91).
- Measured ink in em, base and script (ssty variant) glyphs:
  - x-height letters: base 0.475, script 0.499;
  - ascender letters (b d k): base 0.698, script 0.667;
  - g depth: 0.196 (script);
  - script digits: 0.632;
  - +: 0.58 to 0.60;
  - ∑ (text size): 0.699 up, 0.21 down;
  - √ (20 pt): 0.921.
  - Take these per character from the same Mac Cambria Math the advances came from
    (CambriaMathAdvances.swift): glyf yMin/yMax, plus ssty for scripts. Metrics only.
  - Today Box.inkUp is a flat 0.7 em, and inkDepth is 0 or 0.2.

Checks: the ascent and descent columns are the math line's own (from the gaps, with Calibri 0.9521/0.2686).

## 2. Spacing modes
- **At least X**: line = max(natural, X), with the extra ABOVE. C11, C12 and frac4-10..12 fit within 0.06 (atLeast 30: 27.36 = 27.36).
- **Multiple m**: the equation keeps its natural ascent. The extra goes BELOW and is
  **(m−1) · max(the text's single line, 1.1733 · s)**, where 1.1733 = Cambria Math hhea (1595+455+353)/2048.
  - C10 (2.0, 11 pt): asc 13.50, below 5.49+13.43 = 18.93. Word gaps 29.88/29.40; ours 34.47/35.16.
  - C09 (1.5, 20 pt): extra 11.66 against 0.5 · 23.47 = 11.73.
  - frac3-08 (1.5, 11 pt) fits.
  - This replaces "multiple of the equation" and its even split.
- **Grid (docGrid lines, pitch P)**: H = max(m·P, ceil(natural/P)·P), with the spare split evenly above and below.
  - G2: 26.97/27.04, Word 26.88/27.12.
  - G4 plain 16 pt: 29.06/24.95, Word 29.04/24.98.
  - G5 frac 20: 30.31/23.69, Word 30.24/23.76.
  - G6 (1.5 lines): 36, Word 36.
  - frac4-G4: 28.13/25.87, Word 28.08/25.94.
  - xd 20d9968b n/k line: 17.23, Word 17.31.
  - xd 20d9968b sum+fractions line: 27.58, Word 27.36 (0.22 off: its ∑ is MS Mincho).
  - amd-3's "a simple fraction takes one pitch" rule falls out of this rule
    (n/k is 16.5 < 18; b/g is 2 pitches, G7). Keep the fraction-with-radical-off-grid rule; this lab did not test it.

## 3. Placement inside the equation
- Base-level math runs sit ON the text baseline. Ours puts them 0.2 to 0.67 pt low: frac4-05 0.67; C15 k 0.20 and ∑ 0.48.
- Inline fraction:
  - numerator baseline **1200/2048 s** up, denominator **1030/2048 s** down;
  - bar centred on the axis **585/2048 s**, thickness **133/2048 s**;
  - numerator and denominator at floor(2·s·0.73)/2.
  - Ours is close (0.592/0.5/0.2875).
- Inline ∑ with undOvr limits (Word sets them as scripts beside the sign):
  - The ∑ glyph is centred on the axis: raised 0.04 em at 10.5, 12 and 20 pt.
  - The upper limit stands **SuperscriptShiftUp 750/2048 s** over the ∑ baseline.
  - The lower limit starts **SubscriptShiftDown 418/2048 s** under the ∑ baseline,
    then drops until the ink gap from the upper limit's bottom to the lower limit's top is
    **SubSuperscriptGapMin 300/2048 s**: 1.81 at 12 pt (rule 1.76), 1.61 at 10.5 pt (rule 1.55).
  - The result is 0.40 em over and 0.24 em under the text baseline at all three sizes.
    9b7e4cafe's fixed 0.44/0.195 is wrong at 20 pt: ours puts n at 9.65 over, Word 7.92.
  - Limits are at script size floor(2·s·0.73)/2. Ours uses 14.0 at 20 pt; Word uses 14.5.
