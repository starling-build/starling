# SPEC textCircle (PowerPoint WordArt "Circle") for amd-11 (amd-opus, 2026-11-21)

Source: lab circle2 (47 cells, Arial/Times New Roman/Calibri, squares 120-300,
ovals, 1-3 lines, start angles 180/270/90/30, left/centre/right, long lines,
kerning), PowerPoint print, no repair. Files: /data/s/amd-opus/starling/lab/circle2/
(circle2.pptx, cells.json, ppt-win/circle2-orig.pdf). Scripts: /data/s/amd-opus/wa/
(predict.py = the reference model below, per glyph; c2a.py reads the print).
Positions are read from the PDF text layer (one rigid glyph per Tm) and checked
against the drawn outlines (they agree; the PDF font Widths have no space, use the
face's own space: Arial 0.2778, Times 0.25, Calibri 0.2261 em).

## The rules (all measured; predict.py puts every letter within 0.3 pt in the cells
## the OPEN item below does not touch)

1. THE PATH IS THE PRESET'S OWN, NOT A FULL CIRCLE. presetTextWarpDefinitions
   textCircle: path starts at adj (default 180 deg, clockwise, y down) on the box
   ellipse and sweeps
     adj in (180,360): 2*(360-adj)    adj in (0,180): 2*(180-adj)    adj 0 or 180: 360.
   So the path's middle is always at 0 deg (right side) or 180 deg (left side).
   This is what the current "half" rule approximates; take it from the formula.
2. ALIGNMENT along the path: left starts at the path start, centre is centred on
   the path middle, right ends at the path end. (Verified: left adj 180 starts
   179.6, adj 270 starts -90.2; centre adj 180/270 at 0 deg, adj 90/30 at 180 deg.)
   Current code: right and left use the 0.5 "room" only when centred: wrong, use
   the path length for all three.
3. SHRINK: if the widest line is longer than the path, the whole text (letter
   size, advances, line pitch, inset: everything) scales by path length / widest
   line. Seen exactly: adj 270 (path = half circle, pi*a) 0.805 and 0.895
   (tdf125582 copy). Centred lines at adj 180 up to 0.83 of the full circle did
   not shrink (they are shorter than the 360 path). Current circleFit 0.55 is
   wrong; this replaces it. (Tested with wrap square; a wrap-none line longer
   than its path is untested.)
4. BASELINE of the last line (ring 0): the box ellipse moved INWARD along its
   normal by inset * size (size after shrink). On ovals too: offset along the
   normal, NOT a radial scale (200x100: normal distance 0.193-0.195 sz at every
   letter). Per face: Arial 0.190, Times New Roman 0.183, Calibri 0.152 (drawn
   outlines give the same). Candidate formula, fits all three within 0.002:
     inset = 0.418 - 1.2 * descent/(ascent+descent)   (Windows win metrics)
   Only three faces back it; use it for other faces, measured values for these.
   Current circleInset 0.165 for all faces is wrong.
5. EARLIER LINES: ring k (k lines above the last) is offset OUTWARD from ring 0
   by 1.2*k*size (single spacing). No 4.5 pt extra: current circlePitch
   0.975*size + 4.5 pt is wrong.
6. LETTERS: rigid, at their own size, natural advances WITH pair kerning (kern
   absent/inherited or 1200: kerned; kern="0": not; AVATAR spacing differs
   10%). Each letter is centred on the arc at its mid-advance and turned to the
   curve's tangent there (within 0.2 deg). No extra letter spacing: the current
   circleSpacing / circleSpacingOuter go.
7. SPACING: advances are laid by arc length along the ring's own curve before
   the inset: ring 0 along the box ellipse itself (R = a on circles, arc/advance
   0.99-1.01 on ovals), ring k along the box ellipse offset out by 1.2*k*size.
   The letter's baseline point is then moved inward by the inset (rule 4).
   So on ring 0 the letters are spaced as if on the box edge, slightly tighter
   than their own baseline.

## OPEN (lab budget spent; do not fit a constant for it)

A. THE 0.98 SQUEEZE. In some lines every letter's angle from the path START is
   multiplied by ~0.980 (ring 0) or ~0.966-0.974 (ring 1). Signature: centred
   text at adj 180 sits 3.65 deg early (= 2% of 180), right-aligned ends 7 deg
   short of the path end (2% of 360), left-aligned is barely moved. The radius
   and letter rotations are unaffected. Hit: every ring-0 line in 180, 200 and 220
   squares (all three faces, 24 and 48 pt), ring 1 of the 2-line 140/18pt,
   200/24pt and 300/24pt cells, and both lines of the tdf125582 copy (170 box,
   adj 270, shrunk). NOT hit: 120, 140, 160, 240, 300 squares (ring 0), all
   180 squares at adj 270/90/30, the 200 square at adj 270 shrunk, ovals.
   The cells' markup is identical except the size, so the trigger is inside
   PowerPoint and is a step function of size, not monotonic. Ring-1 0.974 matches
   1/1.026, the control-polygon length of a once-split Bezier quarter arc; ring-0
   0.980 matches nothing I tried. Leave it out (rules 1-7 alone): those lines are
   off by up to ~6-7 pt at their ends, the rest exact.
B. Ring 2 of 3-line cells spaces at ~2.5-2.6 sz out, not 2.4 (radial position is
   2.40). Small: about 1 pt at the line ends.
C. lnSpc other than single, and faces other than the three, are unmeasured.
