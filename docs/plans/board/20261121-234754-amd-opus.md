  p14 （ 730.7 -> 749.4 (PowerPoint 740.5). The extra half-ems are what the ）】 hang was hiding.

## 2. Line-end rule (what the prints show)
- No squeeze. In every ja/zh/ko PowerPoint print, 1,139 marks in fixed-pitch faces (MS Gothic/Mincho, SimSun, Gulim, Batang ...)
  were checked: none is set narrower than 0.8 em, apart from a 1.4 pt thumbnail (rounding) and Batang marks with negative spc.
  Do not build a squeeze. lab hang3 slides 12-14 confirm it (if PowerPoint squeezed, those boxes would hold one line below W0).
- Full hang, 、。，． only: befe9f6b (pilot) p6, justified Meiryo 24 pt: る ends at 894.3, the text edge is 894.71, and 。 stands
  894.8-918.8, a whole em past. The justified line is stretched to the edge WITHOUT the hung 。 (advances 24.1 against 24.0).
  So the hung mark is left out of the width that justify fills.
- No hang, full-width closing marks: lab hang2 (」』）】〕》 and MS Gothic full-width ”’ after kanji, kana or Latin, 20 and 32 pt;
  each goes down with the character before it) and ae6c89bb p2 (」 starting 34.5 pt inside the edge still goes down).
- OPEN, Latin-width closing marks after Hangul: ko-4f5289a9 p6 ")" (Malgun Bold 12 pt) starts 3.3 pt inside the cell edge and
  ends 0.4-1 pt past it. ko-3d95d35b p9 "”" (Malgun 0.379 em) starts 6.3 pt inside and ends 2.6 pt past. Both STAY.
  Two readings fit: (a) a Latin-width mark hangs fully, like 、。; (b) it stays only while its origin is inside the edge (Word's
  hang1 rule for Latin "." "," ")"). lab hang3 slides 1-3, 6, 10 and 11 decide. Until then keep the Body1 pass as it is for ")"
  after Hangul/kana/ideograph. Its "”" entry is WRONG for a full-width ” (hang2: MS Gothic ” goes down), so limit it to a ” narrower
  than 0.75 em (Malgun's 0.379), or wait for hang3.
- Ours also has a bug with "공작 ”" (lab hang3 ours.pdf slide 3): in boxes 40-46 pt wide it puts ” ALONE at the start of line 2.
  Kinsoku forbids that. Fix it with the hang3 result.
