# amd-opus SPEC en 9575f717: Tw Cen MT "pen steps" are print noise; the real miss is a kern pair on a no-break space (for amd-10)

SHORT: no pen-snap rule exists. 9575f717 goes 117/121 -> 121/121 lines with a one-line kern fix.

## 1. The pen steps are not a layout rule
- I rebuilt every glyph's pen position from the print's TJ arrays (Tm + font Widths + adjustments) on all 15 pages and compared it
  with Tw Cen MT's design advances (2048-unit hmtx, /data/corpus-cache/metrics/twcen-20261121) plus its legacy kern pairs.
- The print stays within about ±8/1000 em of that model, and it does NOT add up over a line (line ends -2.7 to -6.4/1000 em).
- No grid: a scan of grid sizes 0.04-0.8 pt finds nothing (best circular concentration 0.28, noise level). Positions are only on
  the PDF's 1/1000 em units.
- Not hinted widths: a per-letter fit over 3,665 positions gives corrections of about ±2/1000 em with mixed signs. hdmx-style
  rounding would give consistent errors of 10+ /1000 that add up.
- The steps sit at TJ array breaks by definition (an adjustment IS an array break), so the chunk hypothesis cannot be tested
  that way. Conclusion: it is the PDF writer's rounding. Do not model it.
- Our line widths on the same text: median +0.02% against PowerPoint, range -0.34% .. +0.33% (81 lines). The old "0.5% wider"
  was fixed by ed8142f15 (kerning).

## 2. The real miss: slide 4, "Global distribution of Candida"
- The caption joins words with U+00A0 ("of Candida", "auris between"). PowerPoint breaks before "of": the no-break
  space after the bold "f" is 0.336 em in the print (8.07 pt at 24 pt), not the space's 0.2661 em.
- 0.336 - 0.2661 = 0.0698 = Tw Cen MT Bold's kern pair f + space (kern-latin-20261121/Tw_Cen_MT_Bold_kern.tsv "0066 0020 0.0698").
  So PowerPoint kerns a no-break space exactly like a space. In this face it has the same advance as the space (tsv 00A0 = 0020),
  i.e. it is set as the space glyph.
- Ours: in RichLayout+Span+StyledSblk2.swift the pair lookup takes the next character only in 0x20...0x7E, so U+00A0 never
  kerns. "of Candida" comes out 1.7 pt short and fits line 1 (ends 959.5 of 960.1).
- FIX (patch at /data/s/amd-opus/starling/lab/nbsp-kern.patch, 1 line): in that lookup, read U+00A0 as U+0020 on both sides of
  the pair. With it 9575f717 is 121/121 (was 117/121). Checked: main 2496e8dd2, full env, test/pptx-line-match.py.
- Scope: this only touches faces with kernsOutsideWord (SlideLatinKerns: Rockwell, Tw Cen MT and their condensed cuts, plus
  Aptos). Run the en and es sets, and any Rockwell deck, before the PR. If a deck drops, check whether that face sets U+00A0 with
  another advance than U+0020 (then it is a separate glyph, and the pair must not apply).
