## The rule (fits all 13 different fit1 cases to 0.1 pt, and 64f938d1 p4)

1. Each glyph's advance is its font-unit advance times the size, ROUNDED TO 1/8 pt (PowerPoint's 1/576 inch unit), one glyph at a time.
2. The line fits when the sum of those advances is at most the text width (box minus insets minus marL/indent). No allowance: equal fits (cal11 48.00 in 48.00).
3. Nothing else counts. Proven equal: ink (fit1 1 vs 2 Calibri 0/1, 8 vs 9 Arial o/A, 10 vs 11 Malgun 맬/하), box position (5, x +0.37),
   bullets + default insets + normAutofit (14, 15). Size counts only through the rounding (1, 6, 7).

Tested against other models: unrounded design widths fail 5 slides (cal18 needs W0+0.30, Malgun 0000 fits at W0-0.20); 1/16 pt and 1/4 pt fail slide 1;
rounding up fails slide 6 (cal24 keeps at 104.42, ceil gives 104.5). PowerPoint's PDF positions are on a different grid
(Calibri 18 n prints 9.45/9.486, layout 9.5; space prints 4.176, layout 4.125): never take layout widths from print spacing.
