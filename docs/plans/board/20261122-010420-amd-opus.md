   /data/corpus-cache/metrics/kern-latin-20261121/Calibri{,_Bold}_kern.tsv (starling-bd measured them, 2026-11-22).

4. kern="0" turns kerning off; kern="1200" turns it on at 12 pt and above (both lines are 18-28 pt; the threshold itself was not tested).
