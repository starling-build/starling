# amd-perf handoff: JPEG re-encode of resized/recoloured photos (PR 1273, engine ed310cc3a0)
- Engine: ImageBridge::JpegCopy(quality) in the engine starling branch (ed310cc3a0). The app finds it by dlsym, so an older engine just keeps the lossless copy.
- App (PR 1273): cache.scaled/recoloured re-encode an opaque result to JPEG at the source's estimated quality; grayscale/duotone/levels pictures (alpha 1, no colour-change key) are baked once and kept JPEG. Alpha pictures are never converted. Copies under 2,600 px stay lossless (PowerPoint's smallest JPEG in the corpus PDFs; a 5x5 JPEG source it writes as Flate).
- A/B (same binary, engine de105acfa2 vs ed310cc3a0): full 212 decks 1172 to 914 MB (70 PDFs change), pilot 46 decks 338 to 299 MB (14), old 555 decks 38 to 28 MB (10). lo-tdf134174 915 to 87 KB, lo-mirrored-graphic 897 to 109 KB, lo-greysscale-graphic 517 to 105 KB. No deck's score moved 0.05 or more (one worst page improved 0.13). 31 sampled Word docs: PDFs byte-identical.
- Trap: the first run with no floor made two full decks (472822baf1a9, eb013aed70e5) 2 points worse: a 50x50 tile re-encoded as JPEG shows blocks where it repeats. The 2,600 px floor fixes both.
- Trap: the sdk keeps a checked-in copy of engine headers (sdk/Sources/FlutterSwiftBridgeCxx/include/engine); a new engine call needs it updated, or dlsym as here.
- Word: Docx.swift has no picture recolour code, so nothing to change there.
- Left: nothing for this task. The old-corpus decks 50-74 had no real gaps (Divino_Revelado is a truncated pptx).
