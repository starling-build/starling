# amd-opus SPEC (Slides puzzle 11), 2026-11-22: is there a hang limit past the text edge? No. For lenovo-2

Data: every corpus deck with a PowerPoint print (pptx, pptx-full, pptx-pilot). Scanners (read-only, reuse them):
/data/s/amd-opus/tools/hangscan3.py (per text box: every line ending in 、。，．,. that sticks past the edge = HANG, every "char+mark pulled to the next line while the char alone would fit" = PULL, plus the box's slack = edge minus the longest ordinary line end),
/data/s/amd-opus/tools/pglines.py <pdf> <page> (line start/end/last glyph width). Output: /data/s/amd-opus/starling/lab/hang/h3.tsv (all), h3f.tsv (validated boxes).
Edge = xfrm right - rIns (inherited through layout/master, group transforms applied). A box counts as VALIDATED when its longest ordinary CJK line ends within 0.5 em inside the edge and none goes past it.

## 1. No distance limit
Validated boxes, left/unset alignment: 88 hangs, spread over the whole range of overhang / font size: 0-0.3 em 12, 0.3-0.6 em 20, 0.6-0.9 em 41, 0.9-1.0 em 15.
A full-em hang is normal: 5cf98ad4 p6 MS Gothic 18 pt "効果的である。" ends 480.3, edge 462.3 (the 。 is wholly outside); the line above ends exactly at 462.3.
So "a hang may stick out at most N pt" is refused by the data. Do not add a limit (it would also be a tolerance picked by score).

## 2. 7d329933 p36: the premise was wrong
The 。 there is Meiryo UI, 10.6 pt wide at 16 pt (print: the lone 。 spans 162.6-173.1), not 16. Line 1 ends 717.1, so the 。 would end at 727.7, 0.14 pt INSIDE the edge 727.8 (box x 155.3 + 579.7 - 7.2).
By the print's own widths the 。 FITS. PowerPoint still breaks before it in all four variants (ea0: 。 alone; ea1: う pulled down, hp 0 or 1). So the question is not "how far may it hang" but "why does PowerPoint's fit test say it does not fit, when the print says 0.14 pt to spare", and then why no hang.
Lead to check first (no lab needed): the line begins "▶︎" = U+25B6 + U+FE0E (variation selector), drawn in a fallback face and absent from the print's text layer; 育 starts at 178.5, so ▶ takes 16.0 in the print. If PowerPoint's layout counts the selector, or measures ▶ in a different face than the one it draws, the line is fuller in layout than in print.
Ours hangs in all four variants, so ours also thinks the 。 does not fit: compare our per-glyph advances on that line with the print's (glyph by glyph, ▶︎ included). If ours over-measures ▶︎+FE0E, fix the width first, then re-check: with a correct width the 。 fits, and our output matches whatever PowerPoint does in each variant except the hang question.
Also note: the orig paragraph has no eaLnBrk/hangingPunct; the text box inherits presentation defaultTextStyle lvl1 (eaLnBrk=0, hangingPunct=0), not master otherStyle (1/1). The print (。 alone) is the settled ea0+hp0 rule, so the orig needs no new rule.

## 3. The other "pull-downs" are mostly geometry, not kinsoku
42 PULL cases. In almost all of them the box slack is about one em (b7d6faa9 p35 21.4 at 28 pt, cd65062f p20 28.0 at 28 pt, 509928fd/7f68063e/87a69a7b/95c41b73 "してみましょ|う。" 17.9 at 18 pt, 805ac359 p30 17.4 at 28 pt). In those boxes no ordinary line gets within an em of xfrm-minus-rIns, so the real text edge is narrower than computed and the "pull" is an ordinary wrap (the pulled char did not fit).
That one-em class is worth a separate look (is it autofit, a right indent, a group or an inherited inset I miss?). It is not part of this puzzle.
Left unexplained, validated boxes only: 87a69a7b p7 (Meiryo 16 pt, explicit eaLnBrk=1 hangingPunct=1, edge 747.9): "が策定さ|れ、" pulled (the 、 would be 9.43 past) while the same box hangs "策定。" 8.53 past and "には、" 2.93 past; 6dccea3b p41 (MS PGothic 32 pt) "まし|た。" pulled at 14.4 past (0.45 em), slack 0.5 em, so its edge is doubtful. One clean case is not a rule: log it OPEN.
