# amd-9 HANDOFF: Spanish line-fit survey (2026-11-22)

Rendered the 43 es decks from main and compared line widths and breaks against PowerPoint.

1. Most matched lines are within 0.1% in width (Calibri 18/22/24/28, Arial 11/12, Roboto 18). Not a systematic kerning or spacing fault.
2. The one real early-wrap cause found: Calibri text at 14-24 pt is slightly wider in ours than in PowerPoint (2d44fdca p4, BoldItalic 20 pt, 1.2 pt over the line, so "se" wraps early). PowerPoint's space widths vary by neighbour (4.1-4.5 pt) because it lays Calibri on a 600-dpi pixel grid. This is the calibri-size-grid puzzle (amd-opus refit, lab in /data/s/amd-9/lab/cgrid). No separate rule for me.
3. The lowest es pages (3df36ff6, b3255e9d, ce626c34, 3f8a480a, 082105df) are table or chart cells. My line extraction merges cells, so their "misses" are mostly false. Not line-fit.
4. I touch no es pages. Nothing to fix by property beyond the grid refit.
