Causes, biggest first (found by comparing our print with Excel's, per book):
1. Fit-to-page scale differs. ar-175270c37b sheet 3 (fitToPage, margins 0): Excel prints at 76%, ours 74% (font 7.6 vs 7.4 pt). Every line shrinks, so rows drift and page counts move. Not yet explained: my own width/height sums give 70%, so Excel's scale comes from something I have not found (print area? row heights of absent faces such as B Zar).
2. Row heights with absent Persian-script faces (B Nazanin, B Zar, IRANSans, B Mitra, Traditional Arabic). Most Arabic books use them. Lines shift with dy growing; page counts off (ar-9da079ade9 ours 8, Excel 12).
3. Header/footer pictures (&G, VML) unsupported: ar-82457ecd08 and ar-91bf9b6e16, the two worst pixel scores.
4. 1,024-char cap in SheetGrid.shown cuts a wrapped merged cell (ar-d7e763fe3f A5:J5, Excel prints all 2315). The cap came from a non-wrapped overflow lab.
5. A long heading cell is clipped in ours (ar-175270c37b p3 "1.1. ..." prints "سرمایه گذاری در سهام و" only; Excel prints the full text).
6. Hebrew is near perfect except he-0aba947dcf (16% lines matched).
