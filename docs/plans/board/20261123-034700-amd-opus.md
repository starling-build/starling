## The rule (fits all five brackets to within 1 degree, no fitted number)

Word does not test the label's box. It tests each text LINE, and it works on a box that is not the one it draws.

1. The box Word measures is single spaced: 15.75 pt a line (21 px at 96 dpi), not the 20.75 pt the 130% spacing draws.
   Read from the down labels, whose foot is fixed: first baselines of 1, 3 and 4 line labels are 31.5 and 15.7 pt apart.
   Each line has its own ink width (no trailing space) and is centred in the box.
2. Along the slice's middle line the box's outer edge stands about 5 pt inside the rim (down: 5.1 for 1, 3 and 4 lines;
   right: 4.3, 4.7, 5.5). It does not move with the slice angle.
3. Across the middle line the box is slid until the text's outline is balanced: the widest angle any line corner makes
   on one side of the middle line equals the widest on the other side. A label of centred lines under the pie stays
   centred. At the right, the 3-line label ("Group 01, age" / "10 to 14" / "14%", a wide line on top) is slid 6.3 pt
   down (measured 6.5 to 7.2), the 4-line one 1.1 pt (measured 0.1 to 0.8), the 1-line one 0 (measured -0.2). This is
   why the inside labels sit off the bisector, the open point in my note 20261123-032100.
4. Inside when every line's corners are within the slice's two edges, i.e. the outline's angle seen from the pie centre
   is no more than the slice angle. No disc test (the outer edge is fixed by 2). The box as a whole may stick out: the
   short top and bottom lines are what count, so 47x77 passes at 44 degrees.
5. Drawing: the text starts at the top of the measured box (first baseline 16.0 pt below it) and runs at the real 130%
   pitch, so it overflows downward: 13 pt past the rim under the pie, and into the lower neighbour at the right.
   That, not a different position rule, is the "displaced toward the bottom" of lab7.

   frame (outer edge by the rim, slide across until balanced), but it is not measured. Free check before landing: lab7
   slide 1 has three inside labels on slanted slices (22%, 15%, 14%); the rule must put them within 2 pt.
b. The outer gap is 4.3 to 5.5 pt at the right and I cannot say why it varies. Use 5 pt; do not fit it.
c. THE LADDER DIFFERS TOO. Word does not take the widest wrap that passes. "Group 01, / age 10 to 14 / pct" (70 wide,
   3 lines) passes this test from 45 degrees down and 42 right, yet Word never shows it: it shows the 46-wide form at
   down 38-54 and right 44-50, and the 54-wide form only at down 56-58. Our ladder (widest line less 1.5 pt, repeat)
   reaches the 70 form first. Word's order looks like natural, then 54, then 46; the step rule is unknown (0.75 or 0.8
   of the natural limit both fit). Also the label that fails keeps its last wrap outside (down 36: the 46-wide form,
   outside, clipped by the frame).
