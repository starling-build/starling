# amd-3: rowformula lab read (Excel Windows print of lab/rowformula, 2026-11-23)

Lab: Latin text, 1/2/3-line rows, 7 faces x 6 sizes (9..16), column width 30, no explicit row heights. Compared Excel's print with ours (bottom-to-bottom of the last line = row height).

1. Row heights already match: Segoe UI, Calibri, Yu Gothic, Malgun, MS Gothic, Meiryo agree to 0.1 pt wherever the line count agrees. The row-height formula (n x R) is right for these.
2. Exceptions where line count agrees but height differs: Nirmala UI Latin text (Excel smaller by 0.6 to 6 pt; per line in 192-dpi px Excel 36.5/41/44.5/49.3/55/62.6 at 9/10/11/12/14/16, ours 38/42/47/50/59/66) and Meiryo 9 (Excel 123 dots a line, ours 128). Our Nirmala table was fitted to Kannada text (zwsp2: 12 pt 3 lines 57.2); Latin-only Nirmala rows come out 1.3 pt shorter at 12 pt. So Nirmala line height may depend on the text's script. Not changed: one lab, Latin-only Nirmala is rare.
3. The bigger gap is WRAP, not height. At 10 and 12 pt (13 and 16 px per em) Malgun, Yu Gothic, Meiryo, MS Gothic wrap one word earlier in Excel than ours (e.g. Malgun 12: Excel 4+4 words, ours 5+3; Excel line width 135 pt vs ours 161). At 9 and 11 pt ours matches. Nirmala 9 and Segoe 9 go the other way (Excel fits 7 words, ours 6). MS Gothic 14/16 and Meiryo 14/16 also differ. Same family as fr-5d2a45c29c (Arial 8 wraps wider in ours) and the Arial 9 question: Excel measures the screen wrap with GDI-hinted advances at the 96-dpi pixel size.
4. What closes it: GDI/hdmx advances (whole pixels) for printable ASCII at 12, 13, 15, 16, 19, 21 px per em (9, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16 pt at 96 dpi) for Malgun Gothic, Yu Gothic, Meiryo, MS Gothic, Nirmala UI, Segoe UI and Arial 8/9 (11 and 12 px). Then a screen-hint table like SheetHinting, applied in the wrap-room measure. Files: lab/rowformula (excel-win/rowformula-orig.pdf), our PDF made with --sheet-pdf.
