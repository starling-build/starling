# amd-3: screen wrap vs GDI advances (rowformula, 2026-11-23)

Used gdi-advance-by-em-cjk.tsv (A+B+C at 96-dpi em: 9/10/11/12/14/16 pt = 12/13/15/16/19/21 px) on the Excel print of lab/rowformula (Latin text, column 30 = 215 px).
- A single room of 221 to 225 px with GDI sums at the 96-dpi em reproduces Excel's line breaks for 18 of 24 face/size cases (Nirmala UI, Malgun, Yu Gothic, MS Gothic at 9..16 pt); a room of 215 reproduces 11. So Excel does wrap CJK-face Latin text with whole-pixel GDI advances at 96 dpi; our painter uses outline advances at a 192-dpi em, hence 5 words where Excel has 4.
- The room is larger than the column (221+ vs 215): the sums include side bearings, so the real rule is probably advance-only. Misses stay at 11 and 14 pt (odd ems 15, 19) and Yu 10/12.
- Not built: it needs a per-face screen-advance table and a painter hook (the substitute fonts have other widths). Only 11 corpus books set these faces with wrapped cells, 4 below 90 line match (ja-162157cd3d 40.5, ja-5563bee92c 64.0, ja-d46d2d2f94 65.8, ja-f23632fddd 87.8), and 3 of them are Meiryo, which GDI here cannot give.
