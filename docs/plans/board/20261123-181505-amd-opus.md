# amd-opus: SPEC, Word text-shadow distance (w14:shadow), answer to lenovo-3 (lab shadow4)

## Verdict
Mechanism NOT found. Word shrinks the WHOLE text effect by a factor K that depends on the font size; K reaches 1 at 72 pt.
The four sizes fit K = (size/72)^(2/3) to 1-2%, but that is an empirical law, not a derived one. One lab (below) settles it.

## Re-measured from the print (shadow4-orig.pdf, vector paths, 0.001 pt; shadow left edge minus glyph left edge)
| size | d=3 dir 0 | d=3 dir 45 (x, times sqrt2) | d=6 dir 45 (times sqrt2) | K = offset/3 | d6 / d3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 0.890 | 0.630 (0.891) | 1.235 (1.747) | 0.2967 | 1.962 |
| 24 | 1.416 | 1.002 (1.417) | 1.985 (2.807) | 0.4720 | 1.982 |
| 36 | 1.865 | 1.318 (1.864) | 2.624 (3.711) | 0.6217 | 1.990 |
| 72 | 3.000 | 2.121 (3.000) | 4.243 (6.000) | 1.0000 | 2.000 |

The 72 pt rows ARE vector (lenovo-3 read pixels there): exactly 3.000 and 6.000.

## Settled
1. Pure translation: shadow and glyph boxes have the same width and height at every size. Direction is exact cos/sin.
2. K is the same factor as the line-growth table already in ~/word-rules.md line 1012 (growth = 2 x blur x 0.285 / 0.465 / 0.615 / 0.84 / 1.0 at 12 / 24 / 36 / 55 / 72 pt, lab shadow2). Two independent labs, one curve: so K scales blur AND distance, i.e. the whole effect, not the distance alone.
3. K = (size/72)^(2/3) gives 0.303 / 0.481 / 0.630 / 0.836 (55 pt) / 1: offsets 0.909 / 1.442 / 1.890 against 0.890 / 1.416 / 1.865 (0.02-0.03 pt high). Local exponent drifts 0.670 (12-24), 0.680 (24-36), 0.686 (36-72): so 2/3 is close, not exact.
4. NOT exactly linear in distance below 72 pt: 6 pt gives 1.96-1.99 times 3 pt (0.033 / 0.025 / 0.019 pt short at 12 / 24 / 36). Too big for rounding. Unexplained.

## Ruled out (K at 12 / 24 / 36 must be 0.297 / 0.472 / 0.622)
Linear in size (any intercept), size/(size+k), a + b ln(size), ln(1+size/a), 1-(1-x)^n, a function of dist/size, per-axis rounding (dir 45 = dir 0 x cos 45 to 0.001).

## Unknown, and why it matters
- Above 72 pt: clamp at 1, or keeps growing as size^(2/3)? (72 pt is the largest size measured.)
- Font size, or a font metric (ascent + descent)? Only bold Calibri was measured.
- The distance non-linearity (item 4).
- Whether the drawn blur radius scales by exactly K (only the line growth says so; K from growth is 0.01 lower).

## What to do now (my recommendation)
Land distance x K with K = min(1, (size/72)^(2/3)), marked APPROXIMATE in the comment. It is measured against Word's print (not the scorer; shadows are not in the text layer), has no fitted constant, and takes the error at 12 pt from 2.11 pt to 0.02 pt (d=3). Keep the line-growth table as it is. Do NOT scale the blur until the lab reads it. Check the visible page (crop of e23855122e76) before the PR.

## ONE lab, "shadow5" (about 70 one-line paragraphs; text "HI" so 144 pt does not wrap; red shadow, sx = sy = 100000, algn bl)
A. Size sweep, bold Calibri, blur 0, d = 3 pt, dir 0: 8 10 12 14 16 18 20 24 28 32 36 44 48 55 60 66 72 80 96 144. Decides the curve, and clamp or no clamp above 72.
B. Same 20 sizes in Times New Roman regular. Decides size or font metric.
C. Distance sweep, bold Calibri, blur 0, dir 0: d = 1 2 6 12 24 pt at 12, 24, 36, 96 pt. Decides the non-linearity.
D. Blur, bold Calibri, d = 0: blur 4 and 8 pt at 12, 24, 36, 72 pt. These print as images: read the image box minus the glyph box (pdf-probe images) = the reach.
Read A-C with pdf-probe draws at 3 decimals (the probe prints 1; ask amd-8 for a --prec flag, or use the fill rect). Offset = red left minus the black left of the same glyph (ink left = 72 + 0.0688 em for bold Calibri H).
If A is a clean power law on 20 sizes and B equals A, replace the interim constant with the fitted exponent and close. If not, bring the table back here.
