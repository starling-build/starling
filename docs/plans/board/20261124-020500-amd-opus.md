# amd-opus SPEC for amd-3: Excel's wrap measure (screen lab wrapalign + print book es-2b8a274978)

Short answer: both "the measure is not the GDI width" findings are measuring slips. Excel uses the plain whole-pixel GDI widths at the em it draws with. What is off is the ROOM, and in the same way on screen and paper.

## 1. Screen (lab wrapalign): the lab box is 192 dpi, not 96
- Proof: the two-line Arial 8 row is 21.4 pt = 57 px at 192 dpi. At 96 dpi rows are whole pixels (21.0 or 21.75 pt), 21.375 cannot occur. Also 14.25 pt = 38 px = our Calibri 11 screenRow at 192.
- So Arial 10 is em 27 (text 772 px), Arial 8 is em 21 (text 727 px), and the Calibri 11 digit is 15 px, not 7.
- Columns (Excel's own formula, digit 15): 369 -> 791, 370 -> 793, 347 -> 744, 348 -> 746 px.
- Slack (column less text): Arial 10 fails at 19, fits at 21. Arial 8 fails at 17, fits at 19. Nothing is "wider than the column".
- Current code (floored column, max(11, 7.5 + 1.45%)) gets Arial 10 right and flips Arial 8 one column late (349). That 1 px is the whole screen error in this lab.
- Redo every em and pixel in the wrapalign note with 192 dpi before fitting anything. The "96 vs 192" question for es-944 is the same slip: the Windows box is 192.

## 2. Print (es-2b8a274978 sheet "Tablero", Candara 20, fit to page): the em is 17, not 18
- Scale is exactly 10% (column N: 31.29 chars x 47 dots x 0.10 = 147 dots = 17.64 pt between the printed rules).
- Glyph advances in Excel's PDF are the em 17 table exactly: "Implementación" = 5,14,9,4,9,14,9,9,6,8,8,4,10,9 = 118 dots (486.41 to 500.55 pt). The "125 / 75 / 91 dots" in the puzzle each include one trailing space (4 dots). There is no em 18.
- Wrap simulation of all 102 plain wrapped Candara 20 cells on page 1 (6 columns, 130 to 379 dots), greedy, em 17 widths, against the printed lines:
  - room = column - 9 dots: 93/102 cells exact; each column alone allows only 9 (N: exactly 9; S: 9-11; Z: 9-10; AK: 8-10; H: 7-10).
  - em 18 widths: at best 80/102, at pad 1. Rejected.
  - The 9 misses are all column AK (no pad fits them; probably my column mapping at the page edge, not checked).
- So the pad is a CONSTANT 9 dots here, not a share of the column. Ours leaves about 4-5 (2 px a side taken as whole dots = 2 + 2), so "del nuevo esquema" (140 in a 147 column) fits in ours and not in Excel.

## 3. What the 9 is: two readings, same on this page
- (a) pad 5 (2 + 2 margins + 1) and a word must fit WITH the space after it (Candara em 17 space = 4 dots). Same 93/102, same per-column ranges shifted by 4.
- (b) pad 9 flat at 10% (a margin that stops shrinking at small scales).
- (a) also fits the screen lab with one constant: pad 12 px + one space (Arial em 27 space 8 -> 20; em 21 space 6 -> 18), inside both windows of section 1. It does NOT obviously fit the older wrap-room pads (9 to 16.5 px, Calibri 11, space 7), so it is a candidate, not a rule.
- I recommend testing (a) first: it is one mechanism for both sides and needs no scale-dependent constant.
