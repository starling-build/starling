## What Word does in the real documents (word-win prints, 110 zh-TW compressPunctuation docs, mid-paragraph lines with pause marks)

- justification: left body 600 squeezed / 1133 not (標楷體); justified 4841 / 6081. Left squeezes about as often as need arises.
- compat mode: 14 (zh-66451627, zh-d45f16c4, zh-340d52e9), none/legacy (zh-31d580df), 15 (zh-db07d44e) all squeeze on left lines.
- grid: zh-31d580df has NO docGrid and snapToGrid=0 and squeezes (p17 "領，檢討項目…驟、": three marks 3.36 pt each at 12 pt).
  zh-d45f16c4 (mode 14, snapToGrid=0, left, body): 110 squeezed lines.
- right indent: 24 squeezed left lines in paragraphs with w:ind w:right.
- face: 新細明體 (PMingLiU) left, mode 14, cell: zh-340d52e9 p6 "□3.燈柱傾斜、燈罩脫落及": 斜 8.88 + 、 9.00 of 11.04 (takes 及).
- which mark: ， 、 。 ： all squeeze.
Exact case of the lab in a real doc: zh-66451627 p2 (mode 14, left, body, 標楷體 14, room 457.9 = 32 cells + 9.9):
  "及時妥善應變處理，基此，本縣…對策，" holds 34 cells, need 18.1. Each mid mark gives 6.12 (3.0 off the character before it,
  3.12 off the mark: the centred mark moves left by half). The TAIL mark gives the same share: 2.64 before it + 3.5 past the margin.
  So on this line (86% of the room used) all three marks give equal shares, tail included. One line; not a threshold measurement.

## Why pm3 says the opposite
pm3's print is the same as pm2's (same lines, same 14.04 on every mark, same file size 36863), and pm2 had NO compressPunctuation.
Word also refused the plain line-end mark squeeze in pm3, which every real doc does. So compression was simply OFF in pm3:
the lab measured "do not compress", not a threshold. "Left-aligned legacy lines never squeeze" is NOT a Word rule; do not gate on it.
What switches it off is something in the hand-made shell (settings/styles/run properties) that every real doc has and pm3 lacks.
Not yet known which; candidates are the items of round 3.
