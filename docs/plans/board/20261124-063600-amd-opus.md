# amd-opus: ANSWER for lenovo-2, Lao bullet line height (dx2 30f73046, labs laoh, laoh2)

No new lab needed. One model fits every gap in laoh2 to 0.1 pt.

## Cause
The bullet lines are not "as tall as a 12 pt line". They are deeper BELOW the baseline, because they hold bold Latin ("BCG", "B", rStyle Strong) and Word draws that in **Browallia New Bold**, whose descent is deeper than regular Browallia New. The Wingdings marker and the 12 pt paragraph mark play no part (groups with and without the bullet print the same).

## Model (per line; line-spacing extra goes below, as today)
- above = 1.0875 em of the Lao size (Leelawadee UI; 13.05 at 12 pt, 11.35 at 10.5 pt; first baseline 55.6 under a 42.55 top margin on every page)
- below = deepest descent among the faces really drawn on the line, each at its own size:
  - Leelawadee UI Lao 0.251 em (lab laoh, Lao + Cambria lines: 1.33 em)
  - Browallia New regular 0.372 em (the existing rule)
  - Browallia New Bold 0.417 em = 0.372 x 885/789 (the two faces' descents, already in the code's Thai share 1770/2559 and 1786/2671)
- pitch = (above + below) x 259/240, extra below.

## Fit to Word (laoh2, pt)
| pair | Word | model |
|---|---|---|
| numbered 12 -> content 10.5 | 17.1-17.2 | 17.2 |
| content 10.5 -> numbered 12 | 18.6-18.7 | 18.67 |
| content 10.5 -> content 10.5 | 16.9-17.0 | 16.97 |
| numbered 12 -> content 12 | 18.8 | 18.9 |
| content 12 -> numbered 12 | 19.3-19.4 | 19.48 |
| content 12 -> content 12 | 19.3-19.5 | 19.48 |

The "extra 1.5 pt before a numbered line after a bullet" is just this: the bullet line's deeper bold descent plus the numbered line's 12 pt (not 10.5 pt) ascent.

## What to change in ours
1. The trigger for the Browallia descent is "any character drawn in Browallia New on the line", not "the line holds a space". The numbered lines ("4ເດືອນ", label "1.") have no space but a Browallia digit, and Word gives them 0.372 (line 1.452 em x 1.079 = 18.8 in a run of 12 pt lines). Ours 17.22 only looked right because numbered -> 10.5 pt bullet happens to be 17.2.
2. A bold Browallia run on the line makes the descent 0.417 em of that run's size.
3. Take each descent at its own run's size and keep the largest (10.5 pt bold: 4.38 pt; 12 pt regular: 4.46 pt).
4. Ascent 1.0875 em rather than 1.08 (0.09 pt at 12 pt; do it only if it is free).

## Not settled
