# SPEC (amd-opus) for amd-1: spaced autofit tables, 3+ columns (dx2 6d16b495, 8be181d6); follow-up to 20261124-060500

Measured on Word's prints of the two dropped docs (compat 15, s = m = b = 15 twips, tcW and tblW auto) and on amd-1's build
(branch w/amd-1/20261124-slab, trace STARLING_RICHTEXT_AUTOFIT=1). Two separate faults; neither is a "grid as slots" case.
Word re-fits these tables: the saved grid is simply Word's own answer, in our slot convention (first 2s+box+s, middle s+box+s,
last s+box+2s; box measured to the OUTSIDE of the cell's own rules). So grid minus gaps gives Word's boxes exactly.

## 1. The room: the gaps number n+1, not n (proven on n = 3, 4, 5, 6)

Word's boxes add up to (T = text width):

| doc, table | n | T - (n+1) x 2s | Word boxes | amd-1 build |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8be181d6 t1 | 6 | 443.1 | 443.2 | 445.5 |
| 8be181d6 t2, t3, t7 | 4 | 446.1 | 446.2 | 448.5 |
| 8be181d6 t4, t5, t6 | 5 | 444.6 | 444.7 | 447.1 |
| 6d16b495 | 3 | 763.9 | 763.1 | 765.4 |

So at compat 15 the slots fill the text width exactly (8be181d6: table frame centre to centre 453.8 on a text width of 453.6),
whatever n is. Ours is 2s (plus rounding) too wide, and the surplus lands on the wide columns: that is all of 8be181d6's loss
(every column of its 7 traced tables is within 0.2 pt of Word except the wide ones, 1.1 pt each).
- Rule for now: room for the boxes = unspaced room - (n+1) x 2s when the document has a compat mode of 15.
- The 2-column lab (compat 12, jc start, no rules) measured unspaced room - 2ns. With n = 2 that is one 2s more. Which of
  compat mode, jc or the rules makes the difference is NOT known; lab tbauto3 below separates them. Until it is back, keep
  the lab form where compat < 15 and use the n+1 form at 15.
- 6d16b495 is a further 0.8 pt (16 twips) short. It has cell rules but no table frame; 8be181d6 has both. Its slots sit
  0.42 inside the left margin and 0.27 inside the right, i.e. about half a cell rule each side. Not proven (s, m and b are
  all 15 twips in both docs, so they cannot be told apart): lab c, e, f, i decide.

## 2. 6d16b495's split: the narrowest of column 3 (this is the 2 and 9 pt)

Word boxes 24.0 / 394.4 / 344.7, ours 22.4 / 387.2 / 355.8. The ordinary sharing gives Word's numbers to 0.05 pt:
  low  = content + 2m + 2b = 18.0 ("p.k.") / 161.9 / 102.6, each + 3.0
  high = 36.6 ("Nr. p.k.") / 1580 / 1580, each + 3.0;  room 763.1;  k = 0.1618  ->  24.01 / 394.4 / 344.7
- Column 2's 161.9 is "būvnormatīva pielikuma 7.tabulā," joined by two no-break spaces (a hyperlink in the middle does
  not part it). Ours agrees.
- Column 3's 102.6 is "15" + no-break space + space + "32.2.apakšpunktu" (12 + 3 + 3 + 84.6), from the text
  "LBN 201-15[nbsp][space]32.2.apakšpunktu". Two things in it:
  a. Word's narrowest PARTS after the hyphen of "201-15" (digit, hyphen, digit). Ours keeps "201-15" whole, which gives
     124.6 and exactly our 31.4 pt split (387.2 - 355.8).
  b. a plain space right after a no-break space is NOT a break place (else the piece would be 84.6, and Word's columns
     would be 24.1 / 401.7 / 337.3).
- So: in _minContent, compat-mode content fit, a hyphen between digits parts; a space that follows U+00A0 does not.
  Check both against lab g and h before landing (predictions below); the fit is exact but rests on one cell.
- Also: the box's low and high each carry BOTH of the cell's side rules (2b) as well as 2m. amd-1's column 1 is 22.4 against
  24.0, so one of low or high is missing them there.
