Three things never change:
1. The picture always stands at y 36: it is measured from where the paragraph stands BEFORE the push. It never moves down.
2. With a full-width picture the paragraph's line always goes under it: baseline = picture foot + 12.8, exactly at the foot, not on the next grid step.
3. One page in every print. The line is never sent to a second page.

The one thing that flips is where the wrapNone boxes are measured from:
- up to h505: from the unpushed paragraph top (36). MINUS49 at 13.3 = the same as with no push. This is the existing rule (word-rules.md "measured from where its anchor paragraph would stand BEFORE").
- from h515: from the PUSHED line top = the picture foot. MINUS49 moves by exactly the picture height (528.5 - 13.3 = 515.2; 532.4 - 13.3 = 519.1).

## The rule

The flip is between 505 and 515. The pushed line is 18 high and the text area ends at 559.3:
- h505: line 541 to 559 fits (559 <= 559.3). No flip.
- h515: line 551 to 569 does not fit. Flip.

So: **when the line pushed under the picture no longer fits in the text area (picture foot + line height > text foot), and the paragraph
is the first on its page so it cannot go to a next page, Word keeps the line on this page past the text foot, and every other float anchored on
that paragraph (not the wrapping picture itself) is measured from the pushed line top (= picture foot + distB) instead of the unpushed top.**
When the pushed line fits, nothing changes from today.

It is not "room on the page" in any looser sense, not the picture height against the text area by itself (505 of 523 does not flip), and not
the wrap kind (topAndBottom gives the same print) or whether the paragraph has text (etxt519 the same).

Honest limits:
- The labs only bound the flip to 505..515; "line does not fit" puts it at 505.3 and is the only natural threshold in that range. Not narrowed further (lab budget used).
- All labs have the paragraph first on the page. With paragraphs before it, Word would most likely move the whole paragraph with its picture to the next page (ordinary pagination); do not extend this rule to that case without a print.
- distB is 0 in every lab, so "foot + distB" is by the existing push rule, not measured here.

## What to implement (amd-1)

Where the empty/first line of a paragraph is pushed under its own full-width square or topAndBottom float:
1. Keep the float where it is (unpushed paragraph top + its offset).
2. pushedTop = float foot (+ distB). If pushedTop + line height <= text foot: today's behaviour, other floats measured from the unpushed top.
3. Else, if the paragraph is first on the page (nothing to move it after): do not page-break; place the line at pushedTop (it overflows the text foot,
   Word draws it), and measure every OTHER paragraph-relative float of that paragraph from pushedTop. Boxes that land past the page foot are simply off the page (Word shows nothing).
4. RuleTrace name suggestion: word-anchor-floats-follow-line-pushed-past-text-foot.
