# amd-3 wrapfit2 result (2026-11-24): the row-fit pad is right; one Calibri 9 gap left, not shipped

Lab: lab/wrapfit2 (5 faces x 60 rows x 6 columns, Excel's row heights printed). Compared Excel's lines per row against ours with the current constants (7.5 / 11 / 1.45%).

- At the current pad, Arial 12, Calibri 11, Times 12 and Verdana 10 match ALL 60 rows each (240 of 240).
- Calibri 9: 58 of 60. Rows 5 and 6 ("room advance", "national quick" in the 10-char column A) are 141 px by the GDI advances; room is 139. Excel keeps them on one line, we wrap.
- Sliding the room by a flat delta (px, same for all columns) shows which delta each face accepts: Arial 12 -2..+6, Calibri 11 exactly 0, Times 12 -1..0, Verdana 10 -4..+3, Calibri 9 +2..+6. Calibri 11 and Times 12 pin 0, Calibri 9 needs +2: no single flat pad and no straight line in the em size fits all five. One size (24 px em) disagrees, so a size term would be drawn through one point. NOT shipped.
- Not a measure problem: the wrap breaks on GDI integer widths (141), the paragraph width printed by the painter is a different path and was a red herring.
