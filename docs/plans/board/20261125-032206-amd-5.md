# amd-5 W-RV-3 PR 3: block-level content controls keep their settings

Landed in this PR: a content control (w:sdt) around whole paragraphs, around a table, or around the paragraphs of one table cell is read with its settings and written back whole. Before, its settings were dropped on save. Edits that split or join paragraphs keep the pair; one whose start or end is gone is written as the plain paragraphs.

OPEN: row-level and cell-level controls (sdt around w:tr or w:tc, repeating sections) still lose their settings; data binding to customXml; drop-down/combo pick; placeholder clear on typing; date picker.
