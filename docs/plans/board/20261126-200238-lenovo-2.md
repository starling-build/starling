# HANDOFF lenovo-2: Slides treemap and box-whisker (chartex)

Brief: treemap and box-whisker charts in Slides (board 20261122-075000-amd-10 called them unsupported).

- Census: no corpus deck holds a treemap or box-whisker; the only chartex deck in the pptx corpus is
  lo-forum-mso-de-138303 (a waterfall). Both kinds were already drawn (amd-10, 11-22). Measured on the lab
  deck chartex-lab2 (/data/s/amd-10/work-bw, PowerPoint's print).
- LANDED (this PR):
  - Treemap strips: squareness is |W/H - 1| (W the box's horizontal extent), not max(W/H, H/W). Fits all 12
    single-level pages. chartex-lab2 mean 1.39% to 1.04%, worst 7.10% (p4) to 2.57% (p8). Grouped pages
    (halving) unchanged.
  - Box-whisker nonoutliers="1": 3 pt dot per distinct inside value, minus one copy of the least and the
    greatest; unstroked, series colour, on the box's middle, drawn before the mean cross. 14 dots, the same
    count PowerPoint draws on p18. The score is unchanged at 2 decimals (the dots are tiny).
  - Save: every chartEx part, style and colours file is byte-identical after a save. Their .rels files lose
    the CRLF after the XML declaration (the writer re-serialises all rels); the content is the same.
- OPEN:
  - p8 (2.57%): item20 and item21 (both 12) are swapped. PowerPoint's order for equal values is not plain
    file order here; one case only, no rule.
  - Box spacing (p17/p18 ~0.97%): PowerPoint boxes 86.2 wide, pitch 95.2, group 2.6 pt left of the category
    centre; ours 86.8, pitch 97.25, centred. Only one geometry (3 series, 1 category, gapWidth 1). Needs a lab
    varying the series count, the category count and gapWidth.
  - meanLine="1" is not drawn. It is unverified: the lab's one category leaves nothing to join. Outlier style
    is also unverified (the lab has no outliers).
