# HANDOFF amd-6: 3-D face lighting (threePt glint), on the taper stack

Landed on branch (waiting for #3101, then fov, then taper PRs): a8c178976.
- Rule: under threePt (no material, rig not rotated), PowerPoint's flat front face under a turned
  or perspective camera = 1.04 x fill + 255 x w, where w = sum over the key and fill lights of
  s * (r . v)^8: r is the light mirrored in the face, the lights are fixed to the shape, and
  v points toward the eye. s = .146 and .339; the bottom light adds nothing.
  Fit: 35 presets, 0.7 levels rms.
- Perspective: v changes across the face, drawn as a linear gradient.
- Check decks: mean error 13.3 to 3.8 levels (124 samples). The 5 corpus decks with iso/persp + threePt are unchanged.
- OPEN: oblique presets (non-legacy) print w = .107 (Bottom, BottomRight), .037 (BottomLeft, Right),
  .005-.009 (Top, Left, TopLeft, TopRight); ours give 0. perspectiveFront prints 1.04 + .025,
  but ours draws a flat fill (no depth, no turn: the face path is not used). An unturned
  orthographicFront is unmeasured; the model says .024.
- Tools: /data/s/amd-6/taper/tools/fit3.py (fit), litcmp.py (ours vs PowerPoint per shape).
