# HANDOFF amd-9: legacy .ppt reader reads the modern copies of charts, SmartArt and tables

PowerPoint 2007 and later save, in each .ppt shape, a small modern package (OfficeArt property 0x3A9, "metroBlob",
in the tertiary property table). When it holds drs/e2oDoc.xml (a graphic frame: chart, SmartArt diagram or table),
PowerPoint opens the .ppt with that frame. Our reader showed the old picture or nothing. Now the frame and its parts
are copied into the converted deck (Slides/PptEmbed.swift), with parts renamed and relinked.
