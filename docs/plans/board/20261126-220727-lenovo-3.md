# HANDOFF lenovo-3: sheet chart value-axis labels (face and baseline)

Landed in this PR:
- A sheet chart's value axis is now set in the face its own txPr names (Excel's print of lab axislbl embeds each named face as itself). The legend still keeps the default.
- The label's baseline stands a measured drop below its gridline, by face, in ems of the label size: Aptos 0.2755, Arial 0.3233, Calibri 0.2889, Cambria 0.3235, Segoe UI 0.3341, Tahoma 0.3472, Times New Roman 0.3148. These are the means of 9, 18 and 36 pt; the spread is 0.005 em or less, and grey and black come out the same. No font-file formula fits all seven (advisor agreed). An unmeasured face falls back to a 1 em box centred on the line with the baseline at the Windows ascent share (about 0.02 em off on the seven).
- Our engine paints a line's baseline at its ascent ROUNDED (Arial 10: ascent 9.216, painted at 9), so the code subtracts the rounded ascent.

Measurements:
- Lab axislbl (7 books x 6 sheets): ours now equal Excel's to 0.001 em on every sheet. Before, every face stood at the default 0.268 / 0.384 / 0.414 em for 9 / 18 / 36 pt.
- 130 chart books (sheets-sweep, line score): mean 75.5 -> 77.0. 26 books are up, 12 are down.
- The drops (chart-tdf124817 91.7->75, chart-dispBlanksAs_2013, chart-title_manual_layout, chart-chart-text-can-overlap, ...) are books whose plot stands 2-12 pt off Excel's. Their labels are now 2.60 pt under our gridline, against Excel's 2.58, so they follow the misplaced gridlines. OPEN: the plot position in those books.

Lab: lab/axislbl (generator /data/s/lenovo-3/charts/mklab.py); measure with /data/s/lenovo-3/charts/axm.py DIR [.pdf].
Not measured yet: bold faces, rotated value labels, the category axis, data labels, the legend. Don't extend the table to them without a lab.
