# amd-2 HANDOFF: tab measuring at a paragraph's trailing whitespace (PR #3195)

Added to PR #3195 (Thai fixes).

1. Tab probe sentinel. A paragraph ending in spaces/tabs: the probe's single line ended in whitespace, and the engine
   measures trailing whitespace from where the last letter ends (Skia skparagraph skips each trailing-whitespace style
   run's width in getBoxes). A word joiner after the text keeps it measured. dx2 e7b500c9's date line: tabs were 10.4 pt
   early; now within 0.2 pt of Word. Rule: word-tab-probe-ends-past-the-trailing-whitespace.
   The engine bug itself is still there (only the probe is affected; final layout is not).
2. The old trailing-tab wrap rule ("restarts-its-stops-past-the-margin") worked partly by accident of that bug.
   It now also covers a body-text tab after a space, starting inside the margin, whose left stop is past it: the
   line breaks at that space (dx2 3578a03d paragraph 44, "คน" + tabs and spaces; Word puts the last tab on its own line).
   Counter-cases kept on one line, matching Word: a tab after a tab (xb ell-z20423699-e882cd header, dx2 5101ed6e),
   and a table cell (xa gv-9fba5bc3b0 "Site Name: \t", 0.8 pt inside the cell edge). The cell exclusion rests on one
   doc; a lab (body vs cell, space+tab near the margin) would settle it.
