# HANDOFF amd-4: W-PG-2 Columns dialog and Line between

What this does:
- Layout > Columns now has One, Two, Three, Left and Right, plus "More Columns…". The dialog sets the number of columns, each column's width and spacing, Equal column width, Line between, and Apply to (this section or the whole document). OK is one undo step.
- `w:sep` (Line between) is read, kept and written. Changed columns are written back as Word writes them, in the last section and in earlier sections' marks. A file's own `w:cols` is kept while the columns are untouched.
- The line is drawn as Word draws it (lab colsep, Word's prints in lab/colsep/word-win on the cache):
  - a filled rule 0.72pt wide, centred in the gap;
  - it starts at the top of the section's text on the page;
  - it ends at the foot of the longest column's last line, without the space after it;
  - where a continuous section follows on the same page, it ends where that section starts.
  - Ours against Word: within 0.07pt across colsep2, colsep3u and colsepmid.
- Fixed on the way: in a document with a single section, unequal columns (`w:equalWidth="0"`) were placed at equal steps, so text and rules sat in the wrong place. colsep3u's middle column was at 290pt; Word puts it at 190pt. They now stand at their own widths.

Left:
- Clicking in a single-section document with unequal columns still finds the column by equal steps (the hit test in RichLayout+Paginate, near `_pxColumnStride`). Floating tables do the same.
- Right-to-left documents with unequal columns still use equal steps.
- Lab question not settled: the colsepmid ending fits "the next section's start" and also "the space after the last line". One lab cannot tell these apart.
