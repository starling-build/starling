# HANDOFF amd-6: S-SH-2 shape adjust handles (yellow)

- Landed in this PR: a selected preset shape shows its yellow handles (every ECMA preset's ahLst, generated by
  test/tools/gen-preset-handles.py from presetShapeDefinitions.xml). Dragging one sets the adjust value to the whole
  number nearest the pointer, within the preset's limits, for turned and mirrored shapes too; polar handles (pie,
  arcs) follow the angle. One drag is one undo step.
- Files: adjust values (`a:avLst`) are read; a change is patched into the shape's own XML (nothing else rewritten);
  new shapes save them. Text stays inside the preset's text rectangle at the new values.
- Fixed on the way: a preset shape written again from the model (for example after a fill change) saved its margins
  plus the preset's text rectangle, so text crept inward on every save (15.98 to 33.56 pt after two saves on a
  rounded rectangle). It now saves the frame's own margins.
- Tests: SlideAdjustHandleTests (9, includes a canvas drag in the harness and every preset's handles placed and
  dropped in place, 200+ handles). Full units 2357, 0 failures.
- Mac driver script: /data/s/amd-6/scratch/ssh2/ssh2.script with OFFICE_FILE=/data/s/amd-6/scratch/ssh2/adjust-drive.pptx.
- PowerPoint check deck: /data/s/amd-6/ui-check/adjust-handles.pptx (six presets with changed values; avLst is
  standard XML, no new markup).
