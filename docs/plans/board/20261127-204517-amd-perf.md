# amd-perf: ribbon group captions fit the band (U-shared)

- Gap: at 1440 the Sheets Home ribbon's Clipboard and Cells groups showed "BOTTOM OVERFLOWED BY 4.0 PIXELS" (macOS debug). Measured on Linux too: each needed 97 px in a 93 px column. Slides Home's Drawing group overran by 12 (105 in 93).
- Cause: a group column holds commands over a 13 px caption in 93 px, so commands get 80. Clipboard stacked four 21 px small rows (84); Cells stacked three 28 px menu buttons (84); Drawing stacked more.
- Fix (shared): a group's commands get the band's room above the caption (RibbonGroup), and a Chrome.rows stack shares that room: no row is taller than its share, gaps keep their size. Writer's stacks already fit and do not change; groups do not get wider. (A first try that started a new column after three rows widened Clipboard, squeezed later groups at 1280 and broke three tests.)
- Test: RibbonGroupFitTests (Writer, Slides, Sheets Home at 1440): sums each group column's children against its height. Fails before (Sheets 2, Slides 1), passes after.
- Driver script: /data/s/amd-perf/scratch/ribbonfit/ribbonfit.script with OFFICE_FILE=/data/s/amd-perf/scratch/ribbonfit/ribbonfit.xlsx.
- Left: the test covers the Home tabs only; other tabs were not swept.
