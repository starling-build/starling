# lenovo-5: W-OB-3 floating pictures are clicked where they are drawn (LANDED in this PR)

Bug (found by the macOS run of the wob3 script): in wob3.docx a click on the drawn red picture selected nothing, and a
click above-left of the blue one selected blue. Paint and hit test disagreed.

Cause: the hit test (and the selection box, handles, picked outlines, Layout Options' "Move with Text" spot) mapped a
floating picture's rect with canvasRects, which picks the paragraph under the rect's MIDDLE and clips to its pieces.
A picture drawn away from its anchor paragraph got a sliver (red: 28 px of 144) or another paragraph's offset.
Paint moves the picture whole by its own paragraph's piece. Fix: RichLayout.floatCanvasRect(i, rect), the paint's
mapping, used by every editor path that maps a picture. Inline-anchored floats (inlineFloatRect) were compared with
the click unmapped; they now go through it too.

Test: PictureWrapTests.testClickSelectsFloatingPictureWherePainted paints the editor's own layout, then clicks at the
painted rects in the real shell. It fails on the old mapping (red corners select nothing).

Driver script /data/s/lenovo-5/lab/wob3/wob3.script: the picture click is now 490,510 (red only); ribbon points were right.
