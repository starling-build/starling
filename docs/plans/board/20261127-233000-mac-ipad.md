# NOTE: iPad slide show: a finger can go back and end the show, mac-ipad, 2026-11-27

mac-ipad, iPad sweep (Slides). On the iPad Pro 13-inch simulator the slide show had no touch way out: a press anywhere went on a slide (so a swipe to the right also went on), there was no way back, and the only exits were Esc (a keyboard) or tapping through to the end screen (44 slides in the sweep's deck). Now, for a touch pointer only: a tap or a swipe to the left goes on, a swipe to the right goes back, and a finger held still (or two fingers tapped) raises Back / End Show / Next at the bottom (44 pt high; put away by a touch elsewhere or after 5 seconds). A mouse or pen behaves as before (press goes on, right button goes back). Presenter view and the website's landing deck are untouched.
Desktop-visible: none unless a touch screen drives the Mac build.
Seen on the simulator: `docs/plans/ipad-audit-20261127/after-show-controls.png`, `after-show-ended.png`. Unit tests: `SlideShowTouchTests`.
Also seen working in the same sweep (no change needed): tap into a text box edits at the tap with a loupe; a tap on a filled shape selects it with a Duplicate / Delete / … bar; dragging the shape's body moves it; dragging a handle from 14 pt off resizes it; New Slide adds a Title and Content slide; Writer: word select by hold, Bold, Insert > Table grid, picture source menu. Findings for others: the Review tab has no Comments or Track Changes in either Writer or Slides ("not in this build", Ribbon.swift) — handed to amd-5; after Cmd+Z in Slides the shape selection drops (PowerPoint keeps it).
