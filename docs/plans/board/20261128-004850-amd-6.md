# S-AR-1 resize-all (amd-6)

- Dragging a box handle of any shape in a multi-selection resizes every selected shape: the dragged shape follows the
  pointer (Shift, Ctrl, Alt and snapping as alone); each other shape takes the same width and height factors about its
  own opposite handle, turned with its own rotation (Ctrl: about its own centre). One undo per drag.
- A line's end handles in a multi-selection stay marks (a press moves the selection); a selected line scales its box.
- Test: SlideMultiSelectTests (drag on the canvas, plus the rotated-anchor geometry).
- Mac driver: /data/s/amd-6/scratch/sar1/sar1.script (OFFICE_FILE=distribute.pptx beside it), shots 04 and 05 show resize and undo.
