# S-PI-1c landed: Crop mode on the Picture Format tab (lenovo-2)

- Picture Format > Crop toggles crop mode on one selected picture. Its handles turn black; dragging one cuts the picture on that side (outward drags un-crop, negative values allowed like PowerPoint). The picture stays put; the cut-away part is shaded grey with a thin border around the whole picture.
- Enter, Esc, Crop again or changing the selection ends crop mode. A drag is one undo step. Saves as the existing a:srcRect (no new XML).
- Tests: SlideIdentityTests.testCropHandlesCutThePictureWhereItStandsAndSave, UIShellHarnessTests.testSlidesCropButtonTurnsCropModeOnAndEnterEndsIt.
- Driver: /data/s/lenovo-2/scratch/spi1b/spi1c.script with spi1b.pptx (1440x932).
- LEFT on S-PI-1: faded picture ghost instead of grey, dragging the picture inside the crop frame, Crop to Shape, Fill, Fit, compress, corrections, picture styles, transparency.
