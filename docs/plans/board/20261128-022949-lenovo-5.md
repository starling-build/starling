# HANDOFF lenovo-5: W-OB-1/2/5 drag a picture or object that stands in the text

Landed (this PR):
- A picture, chart, SmartArt, embedded object or WordArt alone in its paragraph now drags: dropped in the text it
  becomes a character of that line, its old paragraph stays empty (Word's behaviour). One undo puts it back.
  Refused (no change) when dropped on its own paragraph or on another picture's, or while changes are tracked.
- Any drawing In Line with Text drags (was: only wps shapes). A drop in another paragraph now carries the picture
  itself to that paragraph (before, only the character moved and the image was left behind).
- A press that does not travel is still only a click.
- Plan rows W-OB-1, 2 and 5 updated.
- Tests: PictureDragTests (3, harness drags). Full units: 2613, 0 failures.

Left (next, W-OB-3/5):
- A float placed by alignment (wp:align left/centre/right, top/centre/bottom) does not drag: movePicture refuses
  it. Word turns the align into an offset from the same frame on drag. 30 of the 198 corpus docs with anchors have one.
