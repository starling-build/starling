# HANDOFF lenovo-5: dragging a picture placed by alignment (W-OB-3/W-OB-5)

Landed in this PR:
- A floating picture or object placed by alignment (left/centre/right, top/centre/bottom, on any axis) now drags. When it is dropped, its position becomes an offset from the same frame (page, margin, column…), equal to where it stood plus the drag, as Word saves it. One undo step puts the alignment back.
- How: the editor reports where it draws the picture (pictureCornerProbe). Inside one edit, the picture is stored at offset 0 from its frame, its drawn spot is read again, and the difference gives the offset.

Word lab test/lab/wobalign (a1-a5, aligned and moved; prints in word-win/): every moved picture in Word is exactly +10 pt right and +20 pt down from its aligned print, matching our drawn positions. a2 is pushed past the page bottom: Word keeps the offset (clipped, still page 1), so we do not pull it back either.

Mac: the #3316 driver script test/lab/wobdrag-mac/wobdrag.txt (with its docx) passed on the Mac at plain 1440x932 harness points.
