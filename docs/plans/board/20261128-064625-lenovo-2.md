# HANDOFF lenovo-2: S-PI-1e, crop mode shows the cut-away and pans the picture

- In crop mode the cut-away part of the picture now draws as the picture itself, faded (grey until it decodes), with a
  thin outline of the whole picture, as PowerPoint shows it.
- A drag inside the crop frame slides the picture under the frame: what one side cuts, the other gives back; the frame
  stays put. One undo step per drag. Not clamped: PowerPoint lets the picture slide past the frame (negative crop).
- Tests: UIShellHarnessTests.testSlidesCropButtonTurnsCropModeOnAndEnterEndsIt (band, pan, undo), SlideIdentityTests
  (pan arithmetic).
