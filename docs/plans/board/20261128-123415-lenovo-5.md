# HANDOFF lenovo-5: W-OB-5 Chart Design > Change Colors (Word and Slides)

What landed in this PR:
- Chart Design has a "Chart Styles" group with a Change Colors menu: Office's 17 palettes (Colorful 1-4, Monochromatic 1-13). The current one is ticked, and each item shows its first colours.
- Picking a palette gives every series (or every pie slice) its own theme-colour fill. In Word it is written into the chart's own part, so the fill survives a save and a reload. In Slides it goes through the deck model.
- The palettes and their exact fills come from the corpus: 2,398 colour parts and the series fills beside them. The Monochromatic spread is v = (i+1)/(n+1) x 1.4 - 0.7: a shade below 0 (rounded down to a whole percent), a tint above (rounded up). It matches every n checked (2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 31).
- Tests: ChartPaletteTests.
