# Wallpaper reconstruction sky assets

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-20. `sunset-sky.png` is the
initial source; `sunset-sky-v2.png` is the selected environment texture, edited
from that source. Both are 1774 × 887 pixels. These are sky-only illustrations,
not copies or projections of the city wallpaper. The geometry remains 3D.

The generator decodes sRGB to linear light, adds a small analytic sun aligned to
the reference view, and uses Filament cmgen to bake the skybox and reflected
lighting. Water normals are deterministic periodic wave derivatives generated by
`water_normal()` in `build/tools/wallpaper-city.py`.

Sky mood and palette like a beautifully rendered detailed voxel San Francisco wallpaper at warm sunset, but clouds are naturally soft and delicately textured. Technically usable seamless equirectangular projection: left and right edges match, poles uniform. No frames or watermarks.
