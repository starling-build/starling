Lab bal -- Word for Windows' markup balloons: layout of a deleted section break, a deleted mark that keeps inserted text, two balloons at one anchor, runs of reformatted paragraph marks (Linux lo5, 2026-11-02)
Background: LibreOffice's n830205 (lo-9f27ef40, in the 756 set too) prints a column of balloons ("Deleted: XXX¶", "Formatted: Font: 9 pt", "Deleted: " + "Section Break (Continuous)") that ours gets right in kind and wrong in order and layout in 14 lines. Questions: (1) how a deleted continuous / next-page section break is drawn: Word's print has "Deleted: " and "Section Break (Continuous)" side by side on one line (x 437 and 498) with a second, smaller (5.3pt) "Section Break (Continuous)" under it at x 489. (2) a deleted paragraph mark whose paragraph keeps INSERTED text: does it get its own "Deleted: ¶"? (3) two balloons on one anchor: deletion + formatting, comment + deletion, either order: which stands first? (4) paragraph marks reformatted alike in a row: one balloon, where? and with a plain paragraph between?
Every document: Letter, 1in margins, Arial 9, paragraphs "LINE n ..." around the change. Print with Word for Windows exactly as the corpus prints are made (all markup shown, balloons in the right margin).
Names: bal-01 del-text-mark, 02 del-sect-cont (empty paragraph with a continuous section break, mark deleted), 03 del-text-then-sect, 04 del-sect-next, 05 del-mark-ins-text, 06/07 del-and-fmt-same / fmt-then-del-same, 08/09 comment-and-del / del-and-comment, 10 marks-fmt-x4, 11 marks-fmt-x2-gap, 12 fmt-only-run-then-mark.
usage: build.py OUTDIR regenerates docx/. analyze.py PDF-or-DIR > table (T = text line, B = balloon line: x, top, bottom, size, text). Send the table on the branch (BAL-WORD.tsv).
What matters: the order and position of every B line against the T line of its change; which B lines share a y.
