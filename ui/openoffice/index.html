Starling Office

Big ideas. Start here.

Writer, Slides, and Sheets. An open source office suite for documents, presentations, and spreadsheets.

Writer — write something great.

A familiar ribbon. A clean page. Fonts, styles, tables, and pictures. Open and save DOCX, RTF, Markdown, and plain text. Open Writer.

Slides — make your point.

Build presentations with themes, shapes, tables, charts, and animations. Present with speaker notes. Open and save PPTX. This page itself is an editable presentation rendered by Starling Slides. Open Slides.

Sheets — let the numbers tell the story.

Organize data with formulas, tables, sorting, and filters. Work across multiple sheets, format cells, and open and save XLSX and CSV. Open Sheets.

Your office. Everywhere.

The platform roadmap spans macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android, and the web.

Open code. Shared ambition.

Writer, Slides, and Sheets are built on the Starling SDK. Apache 2.0 licensed. Explore the project.
